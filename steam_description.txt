Banking Nation Improvements
Version 3.0.0 | Compatible with EU5 1.4.7

Rebuilds the Banking Nation government around one loop: money buys agreements, and agreements bring in more money or influence over the countries you deal with.

Version 3.0 is a ground-up rework and is NOT compatible with saves from 2.x. The Creditor's League, the Realm in Arrears subject type and most of the old buildings and interactions are gone, replaced by a smaller set of agreements that fit together. More are on the way; please leave feedback.

---

DEBT

- Take Over All Loans - buy up every estate-held loan a country owes and collect the interest yourself.
- Buy Out Bonds - pay off a country's government bondholders. Its open-ended bonds become ordinary 10 year loans owed to you, at the same interest.
- Lending Rights - pay a country a signing sum; from then on every new loan it takes from its estates is held by you instead.

---

AGREEMENTS

Each agreement is signed with one country, costs a signing sum up front, and lasts until either side ends it or you go to war with each other. You can hold several over the same country.

- Tax Farming - collect a tenth of the country's tax income every month.
- Trade Factorage - your agents run the country's commerce. It trades more efficiently, and you take a tenth of its trade income every month.

---

WAR

- Hire War Ally - banks have no armies, but gold buys swords. Pay a country to join one war you are leading. When the war ends, so does the arrangement.
- Sway Their Vote - bribe international organisation voters with gold alone, with no prestige cost.

---

BUILDINGS

- Banking Office - back to its vanilla form.
- Banker's Kontor - bank-owned merchants trading on your behalf.
- Banker Shipyard - build ships in a foreign port (with local permission).

---

GOVERNMENT

- The Trading-Focused and Financial-Focused sub-reforms are merged into the base Banking Family reform, which now grants extra trade income and a more content, less powerful burgher estate.
- Cabinet actions: Expand Diplomatic Network, Monopolize Local Commerce.
- Absorb Foreign Branch - annex a subject bank and take over its network.

---

COMPATIBILITY

Patches these vanilla definitions: banking_country and bribe_voter_for_policy. The on_loan_taken on_action is appended to, not overwritten. No other vanilla files are touched, so it should work with most mods unless they patch the same things.
