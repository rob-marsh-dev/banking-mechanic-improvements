Banking Nation Improvements
Version 2.1.0 | Compatible with EU5 1.3.8 (Open Beta)

Expands the Banking Nation government into a full playstyle built on economic dominance, diplomatic leverage, and debt-based power projection.

Landless nations are in a rough spot right now, so some liberties and ongoing tuning are needed. Please leave feedback so I can keep improving the mod.

---

CORE CHANGES

- Banking Office: 3 levels (vanilla: 1), buildable outside towns, now an expensive high-impact building with steeper per-level costs.
- Bank Maintenance: production method retuned to reflect the real cost of running a bank.
- Two government sub-reforms: Trading-Focused (trade income & efficiency) or Financial-Focused (cheaper foreign building & upkeep).

---

THE CREDITOR'S LEAGUE

A new international organisation exclusive to banking nations. The founding bank is permanent leader; members gain small economic bonuses, the leader gains rewards that scale with membership (merchant capacity, max diplomats, diplomat generation).

- Member market loans are financed from the leader's treasury — the debt, and its interest, is owed to the bank.
- Invite nations diplomatically; wealth and size raise resistance, while heavy debt (especially debt the bank already holds) and prior investment make them willing.
- Members may request a credit line directly from the bank.

Parliament — the Council of Creditors votes on nine laws:
  Payment (subsidies vs. escalating levies), Construction (build League buildings in member lands), Military (recruitment & naval access), Intelligence (spy-network sharing), Manpower Levy, Sailor Levy, Internal Relations (peace vs. open war), Subjugation (restrain members from taking their own subjects), and External War (from free hand up to full submission).

League War: the Council may vote to compel an outside nation by force. On a majority, the bank leads the war. Five-year cooldown per vote.

---

NEW BUILDINGS

- Currency Exchange — trade income and regional commerce influence.
- Banker's Kontor — bank-owned merchants trading on your behalf.
- Banker Shipyard — build ships in foreign land (with local permission).
- Bank Recruitment Firm — recruit bank-loyal regiments abroad (with local permission).
- Art Emporium — cultural prestige and reach.
- Banking Mining Interest — squeezes value from resource-rich locations.
- Family Factor House — a family factor seated at a foreign court, in the room when the throne needs a loan.
- League Buildings (built in member territory with Construction Rights): Trade House, Counting House, Credit Office, Endowed College.

---

SUBJECT TYPES

Realm in Arrears — a debtor placed under direct financial governance, granting the bank the right to recruit regiments and build ships in its territory.

---

DIPLOMATIC INTERACTIONS

- Absorb Foreign Branch — consolidate another bank's branches into yours.
- Pressure Into Loan / Strongarm Into Fees — push a nation into debt or worse terms.
- Seize Crown Assets — claim collateral from a debtor.
- Take Over Loan / All Loans — buy up debt at principal, keep the interest as profit.
- Foreign Investment in Administration — buy into a rival's government for a scaling economic penalty and a monthly chance to force them into bank-owned loans, softening them up for League membership.
- Buy Sovereignty — pay an overlord to release a League member from vassalage.
- Sway Their Vote — bribe IO parliament members with gold instead of prestige.
- Cabinet Actions: Expand Diplomatic Network, Monopolize Local Commerce.

---

COMPATIBILITY

Patches these vanilla definitions: banking_country, banking_office, bribe_voter_for_policy, and monthly_country_pulse (on_action, appended not overwritten). No other vanilla files touched — compatible with most mods unless they patch the same.
