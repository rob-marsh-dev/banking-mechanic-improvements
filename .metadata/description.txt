Rebuilds banking nations around agreements. Money buys agreements, and agreements bring in more money or influence.

- Take over a country's loans and buy out its government bonds
- Lending Rights, Tax Farming and Trade Factorage agreements
- Hire countries to fight in your wars
- Banker's Kontor and Banker Shipyard foreign buildings

Version 3.0 is a rework and is not compatible with 2.x saves.
