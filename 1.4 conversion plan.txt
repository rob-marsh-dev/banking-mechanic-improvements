Idea: mod is kind of a patchwork of differing systems, design ideas, and very VERY ahistroical. The goal is to try the bring the system together, into a more unified whole.

Core thoughts:

Buildings:
The bank buildings and trade post work well, for the moment. I think reducing them back to their vanilla price range and benefits is probably for the best, as they are just profit machines. We want the player to interact with countries to make more money, not sit on buildings.
Manpower and Sailor buildings need a looking at. Not entirely clear what purpose they serve. Decision: cut the manpower building.
KEEP the shipyard. The bank owning ships makes sense, and the foreign shipyard is what lets the player build ships there.
Influence buildings need a serious combing. They offer easily stackable static benefits, meaning things like +120% trade share start to become possible. no bueno
Plan for influence buildings: they raise the bank's influence in the HOST country only. No global static bonuses, so nothing stacks.

SEPARATE from the influence buildings: I would think it would be really cool if we could maybe make a set of buildings, such that they are productive for the nation that hosts them. These boost the local economy of the city they are in. Thinking Victoria 3 foreign investments: the bank buys/builds the building, and its profits flow back to the bank.

Reforms:
Consolidate the Fianance/Trade focus banks into simple changes to the base Banking Family reform. 
These should be things like handling the burgher estate. Reduce interest modifiers, give a larger base chunk of trade revenue. Burgher relations? i mean they are basically employees

Relations:
remove ALL those stupid one off interactions. these are basically just bloat and do nothing.
get rid of the spy actions as well. They serve little purpose.

Creditors Union:
remove this dogwater IO. its extremely hacky, ahistorical and unfinished. Maybe replacing with something.

Loan interactions:
Keep the scoop up all loans button. look into fixing the displayed text. 
Bonds. bonds bonds bonds. something i NEED to interact with but cant. idea: buy bond interaction. purchase using the same math the loan takeover uses. then, delete bond from country. agreement is set up in so much that the bond payments are then funneled over to the buying bank nation. regarding repayment, the math the AI uses to pay back bonds should be used to make the AI payback the bond to the bank. This should allow us to do things like force minimum repayment times, etc.

IDEAL FLOW:

here is my idea for how i think gameplay should go.

AT ALL TIMES: making money. We need money to make money, and we spend that money on influence.

Money -> agreements -> more money or influence.

Agrements increaes either the political power of the bank, or make the bank more money. 

Money is simple. Lump sums, maybe some other kind of payments to add other costs to spice up gameplay.

Influence, on the other hand, is more complicated. Things like getting better trade status, allies, manpower, etc. everything a landed nation can do the bank should be able to do through costly agreements. 

We use influence in two other ways.
One is pretty standard. Allies, Navies and Armies build up and the bank can start to do things like war. The bank having a standing army is ridiculous, it should basically be limited to mercs. Navy is different: it makes sense for the bank to own ships (merc ships exist in EU5 too). Add customs CB's that maybe force agreements, MAYBE, MAYBBEEEE subjects. I really dont like the subjects idea though.

The other, is replicating the medici in Florence. As influence builds up, gain more reasons and/or reduce costs to basically install a banking family member on the throne. This should be kind of a climax, or crescendo, to the interactions with a country. I think, this should replace the Creditors Union. where the bank is the leader of the IO and LANDED nations are members. We can add policies here for different things.

in closing:

money -> agreements, buildings -> more money -> influence over neighbors -> political agreements -> internal agreements -> dethroning nations -> new IO
